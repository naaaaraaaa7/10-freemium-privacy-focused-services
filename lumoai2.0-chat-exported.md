## 📝 Notes

- Jurisdiction matters: EU-based services (Germany, Belgium) offer stronger legal privacy protections than US-based ones
- Open-source tools are generally more trustworthy as they can be independently audited
- Free tiers typically have limitations compared to paid versions (storage, features, device limits)
- Always verify the current offerings as free tier policies may change over time
